Welcome to my portfolio website! My name is Vincent and I make games. After finishing my Game Design studies in 2023 I have developed and published my strategy game Konkwest. In October 2024 I have joined Aquila Interactive: a fantastic, global team working on a new grand strategy game: Gilded Destiny.

Games and computer technology have been my passion for as long as I can remember. It started when I collected and hand-crafted trading cards in school, and I have therefore styled this website in reference to my fondness of card games.
